
# 04 – Project Planning

The planning phase focused on translating the project objectives into actionable tasks, establishing a realistic timeline, defining quality standards, and preparing a customer feedback mechanism.

As the Project Manager, I organized the activities required to prepare for the pilot tablet rollout across the North and Downtown locations.

## Planning Deliverables

### 01. Task Brainstorm
Identified and organized the activities required for the project, including initiation, vendor selection, menu design, staff training, tablet installation, system integration, testing, and pilot readiness.

### 02. Project Timeline
Developed a 12-week project schedule starting June 1, 2026, outlining task durations, start and due dates, task owners, dependencies and key milestones. The pilot launch target was August 11, 2026.

### 03. Quality Plan & Evaluation
Defined quality standards and evaluation measures for customer experience, order accuracy, service time, checkout efficiency, and technical reliability.

### 04. Customer Survey
Prepared post-dining survey questions to collect feedback on tablet usability, ordering experience, technical issues, staff assistance, checkout, and overall dining experience.
