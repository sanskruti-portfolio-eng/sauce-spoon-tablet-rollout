
# 02 – Project Overview

## 1. Business Need

Sauce & Spoon aimed to improve restaurant service efficiency, enhance the customer dining experience, and support its annual business growth goals. The existing ordering process presented opportunities to improve order accuracy, reduce service delays, manage food waste, and gain better insights into customer preferences and sales.

To address these needs, the business proposed introducing tabletop menu tablets as a pilot initiative.

## 2. Project Purpose

The purpose of this project was to plan and coordinate a pilot rollout of tabletop menu tablets in the bar sections of Sauce & Spoon's North and Downtown restaurant locations.

The tablets were intended to streamline customer ordering, integrate with the existing host and Point-of-Sale (POS) systems, promote menu add-ons and featured items, and provide operational data to evaluate the effectiveness of the solution before considering a wider rollout.

## 3. Project Objectives

The key objectives established for the pilot were:

- **Tablet Implementation:** Deploy operational tabletop menu tablets at the North and Downtown locations.
- **Improve Service Efficiency:** Reduce average table-turn time by approximately 30 minutes.
- **Increase Average Check Value:** Increase the average customer check from $65 to $75 through tablet-based recommendations.
- **Grow Appetizer Sales:** Target a 10% increase at North and 20% at Downtown.
- **Increase Guest Count:** Achieve a 10% increase in average daily guest count at the pilot locations.
- **Reduce Food Waste:** Target a 25% reduction in food waste.
- **Promote Product Mix:** Encourage purchases of appetizers, specialty beverages, selected entrees, and additional menu items through tablet recommendations.

## 4. Project Scope

**In Scope**
- Tablet implementation in the bar sections of North and Downtown.
- Integration with existing host and POS systems.
- Digital menu configuration, add-on recommendations, and coupons.
- Staff training and operational readiness.

**Out of Scope**
- Replacement of existing host or POS software.
- Tablet rollout across all Sauce & Spoon locations.
- Tablet implementation outside the designated pilot bar sections.
- Changes to the existing order-return policy.

## 5. Expected Business Benefits

The pilot was expected to:
- Improve customer ordering experience and service efficiency.
- Reduce order errors and food waste.
- Encourage additional purchases and improve average check value.
- Provide useful sales and operational data for business decisions.
- Help leadership evaluate the feasibility of expanding the tablet solution.
