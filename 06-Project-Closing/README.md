
# 06 – Project Closing

The project closing phase focused on reviewing the overall project delivery, evaluating outcomes against the intended objectives, documenting lessons learned, and capturing information to support future implementations.

As Project Manager, I consolidated the project outcomes, reviewed customer and stakeholder feedback, and documented the closeout findings and project impact.

## 01. Project Retrospective

The retrospective captured what went well, identified challenges, and documented improvement actions based on customer feedback and the test-launch experience.

Key takeaways included:
- Positive customer acceptance of tablet ordering.
- The importance of staff guidance and customer support.
- Opportunities to improve tablet navigation and order accuracy.
- The need for reliable devices and better troubleshooting procedures.
- Improvements to customer engagement and restaurant operations.

## 02. Project Closeout Report

The closeout report summarises the project journey, implementation activities, delivered scope, project results, schedule, and cost information.

## 03. Project Impact

The pilot established tabletop tablet ordering at the North and Downtown locations, supported staff readiness, and provided customer feedback to guide future improvements.

The project impact review highlights:
- Customer acceptance and checkout experience.
- Technology implementation and operational readiness.
- Order accuracy and technical reliability.
- Customer engagement and improvement opportunities.
- Lessons for future tablet rollouts.
