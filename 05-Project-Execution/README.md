
# 05 – Project Execution

The execution phase focused on implementing the tabletop tablet solution, preparing restaurant staff, conducting test-launch activities, and evaluating the customer experience.

As Project Manager, I coordinated the pilot activities and reviewed customer feedback to identify successes, operational challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

## Project Deliverables

### 01. Quality & Evaluation
Defined quality standards, evaluation criteria, and measurable indicators for customer experience, order accuracy, service time, checkout efficiency, and technical reliability.

[View Quality & Evaluation](01-Quality-and-Evaluation.pdf)

### 02. Customer Survey Questions
Prepared a customer feedback questionnaire covering tablet usability, ordering experience, technical issues, staff assistance, checkout, and dining satisfaction.

[View Survey Questions](02-Customer-Survey-Questions.pdf)

### 03. Customer Survey Results
Analysed feedback from 50 test-launch customers to evaluate tablet acceptance, usability, checkout experience, order accuracy, and technical reliability.

[View Survey Results](03-Customer-Survey-Results.pdf)

### 04. Project Retrospective
Reviewed customer feedback, documented what went well, identified improvement areas, and proposed actions for future implementation.

[View Project Retrospective](04-Project-Retrospective.pdf)

**Key Focus:** Implementation, operational readiness, customer feedback, quality evaluation, and continuous improvement.
