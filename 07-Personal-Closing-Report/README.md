
# 08 – Personal Closing Report

## My Reflection

Completing the Google Project Management Certificate and its capstone project gave me an opportunity to apply project management concepts to a realistic business scenario.

Through the Sauce & Spoon case study, I took on the role of Peta Tsosie, Project Manager, and worked through the project lifecycle — from initiation and planning to implementation, evaluation, and project closing.

## 1. Key Accomplishments

- Developed a project charter with SMART goals and stakeholder analysis.
- Created a structured task brainstorm, project timeline, and quality evaluation plan.
- Prepared customer survey questions and analysed feedback from 50 customers.
- Documented project outcomes, retrospective findings, and closeout activities.
- Organized the project deliverables into a structured professional portfolio.

## 2. Lessons Learned

- A project is not complete simply because the deliverable has been launched; proper documentation and handover are equally important.
- Effective communication and collaboration are essential when coordinating cross-functional teams.
- Customer feedback and retrospective reviews help identify issues that may not be visible during planning.
- Project schedules must account for dependencies, resource availability, and unexpected delays.
- Stakeholder engagement and clearly defined objectives provide direction throughout the project.
